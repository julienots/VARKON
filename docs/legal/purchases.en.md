# Purchases and refunds

## This build
No real-money purchase is configured. The "Crystals (real money)" shop section shows those products as unavailable: no payment is possible or simulated.

## In-game currencies
Gold, crystals, shards, components, mutant essence, war tokens and ancestral materials are earned by playing (battles, missions, achievements, pass, buildings). The pass premium track is bought with crystals, which can be earned for free.

## Future build with real purchases
If real purchases are added, they will go exclusively through Google Play Billing, with server-side validation, clear prices and restore of eligible purchases. Refunds will follow Google Play's official procedures (Google Play help > Request a refund). No automatic refund is promised.
