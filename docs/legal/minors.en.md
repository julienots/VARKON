# Protection of minors

- No real-money purchases in this build.
- In-game prices are always shown before buying, with a confirmation (can be turned off in settings).
- No misleading timers or fake discounts: daily offers really change at midnight (device time).
- Chest odds are displayed and computed from the tables actually used.
- The whole campaign can be finished without spending real money.
- Before any release with purchases, SuperEssence Studio must check the obligations of target markets (PEGI/IARC rating, local loot-box rules).
