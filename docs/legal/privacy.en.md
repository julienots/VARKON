# Privacy policy — VARKON

Publisher: SuperEssence Studio. Document version: 0.9 (test build).

## Data collected
This version of VARKON runs entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no online account and no payment module.

## Data stored locally
On your device only, the game stores:
- your progress (level, cards, Goblin King, base, inventory, missions, pass) in a save file and a backup copy;
- your settings (graphics, audio, accessibility, language).
The player name you type stays on the device.

## Purpose
These files are only used to keep your progress and settings between sessions.

## Retention
Data stays on the device until you erase progress (Settings > Account), clear the app data or uninstall the game.

## Your rights
Since no data is sent to SuperEssence Studio, we hold no data about you. You can delete your local data at any time from the game (Settings > Account > Erase progress) or from Android settings.

## Android permissions
The game requests no sensitive permission (no location, contacts, camera or microphone). Vibration is only used when enabled in settings.

## Changes
If a future version adds online services (cloud save, multiplayer, purchases), this policy will be updated before they are enabled and will describe the data involved.
